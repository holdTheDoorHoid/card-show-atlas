# Brief: region sweep

Read `_common.md` first. Its rules always win.

You sweep one region (a few states or provinces) for card shows of every size from now through
2027: the monthly show at a VFW or fire hall, mall shows, fairground shows, TCG shows at hotels, and
the big expo center shows. Small shows matter most here, because nobody else lists them well.

Your prompt lists shows that are ALREADY KNOWN for your region (from earlier waves). Do not research
those again unless you find new dates or vendor info for them; spend your effort on new shows.

## How to find shows

Use your WebSearch budget on searches like these, varied across your states and their biggest cities:

* `"card show" <state> November 2026` (and December 2026, January 2027, spring 2027)
* `"sports card show" <city>` and `"pokemon card show" <city>` and `"TCG show" <city>`
* `"card show" fairgrounds <state>` and `"card show" "expo center" <state>`
* `"card show" VFW OR "fire hall" OR "Elks" <state>`
* `"collectibles show" OR "trading card show" <state> 2027`

From each result, go to the **organizer's own page** (or the venue's event page, a local news item, a
city or tourism calendar, a library or church calendar). Snippets from listing sites in the
never fetch list are leads: use the show name to find the organizer's page with your next search or
by guessing the obvious site. A promoter with one show often runs several: check their schedule page.

Venue calendars are gold: when you find a fairground, expo center or hall that hosts card shows,
read its event calendar page for all of them.

## Write

Your output path is given in your prompt. Put every show you saw but could not confirm (Facebook only,
listing site only) in `leads_only` with the name, city, date you saw, and where you saw it. Those
become future research and organizer outreach.
