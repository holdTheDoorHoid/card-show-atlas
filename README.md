# Card Show Atlas

Find trading card shows near you, anywhere in the US and Canada. Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh, One Piece,
Lorcana, sports cards and everything in between.

**Site:** https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/card-show-atlas/ (early preview, shows are still being added)

## What it does

* **Find shows near you.** Enter a ZIP or postal code and how far you will drive, and get a list.
* **Browse the map.** Zoom anywhere and drag the time window to see what is on in any week.
* **Plan as a vendor.** Track shows you are watching, applied to and booked, with table prices and
  application deadlines, and export the deadlines to your calendar.
* **Plan a route.** Hit several shows in one weekend, or pick the best set of shows across a season,
  with an estimate of what each plan costs and might earn.

Everything you save (your location, your shows, your plans) stays in your browser.

## Add or fix a show

* No account needed: use the form linked on the site's "Add a show" page.
* Have a GitHub account: [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose).
